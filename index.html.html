I'm Sorry, My Love.

I know that saying “sorry” doesn't magically undo anything. So I wanted to give you something more than just two words.

♥
My love,

I’ve been thinking about what happened, and honestly, I know I could have handled things better. I may not always find the right words, and sometimes my actions can hurt you even when hurting you was never my intention.

I’m not writing this to make excuses or to prove that I was right. I’m writing because you matter to me, and your feelings matter to me too. If I made you feel ignored, unloved, disappointed, or alone, I am genuinely sorry.

I wish I could go back and change the moments that hurt you. I can’t change them, but I can learn from them. I want to listen better, communicate better, and become someone who makes you feel safe and loved — not someone you have to beg for understanding from.

I don't expect one letter to fix everything. I just want you to know that behind my stubbornness, silence, and imperfect way of showing things, I really do love you.

I'm sorry. Truly. ❤️
